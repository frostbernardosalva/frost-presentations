Typography

Sailec

Light Regular Medium Bold

One family for everything — display, body, navigation, labels. No second face, no mono. Display carries negative tracking: 80px/-1.44px at hero, 40px/-1.2px at page headline. Body sits at 16px/22px, normal tracking.

Geometry & components

Work with Frost

  • Radius 45px pill, or ~3px. Nothing between.
  • Button #0B1464, white, Sailec 700, 14/24/12px.
  • Fields no box — one 1px rule in #2E3949.
  • Surfaces dark-first; shadows essentially unused.
  • Eyebrow 12px, 700, uppercase, 0.18em, in blue.
